Virtual Reality (VR) is a technology that immerses users in a simulated digital environment. By using VR headsets and sensors, you can see, hear, and interact with 3D worlds as if they were real.

VR is used in education, design, healthcare, and entertainment. It helps people learn, create, and experience things that are impossible or impractical in the real world.

People say VR makes them feel like they are truly inside the digital world. It turns learning, gaming, and exploring into exciting experiences. Users love the interactivity and immersive feeling VR provides
